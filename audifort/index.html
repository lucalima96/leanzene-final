Scientific Evidence for Key Ingredients

Below are peer-reviewed studies suggesting that these ingredients may support hearing and nerve health. Individual results may vary. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

Japanese Alpine Ginkgo Biloba

  • Study on the positive impact of alpine ginkgo biloba on auditory function. Journal of Food Composition and Analysis
  • Trial with 547 participants living with tinnitus and hearing difficulty reported improvements in auditory function. Clinical trial
  • Research suggests ginkgo may support blood circulation from the inner ear to the brain. PubMed

HydroPure NAC (N-Acetylcysteine)

  • 2023 study reported reduced ringing and improved sound clarity within seven days. Journal of Otology and Neurotology (2023)
  • Clinical trial: participants detected subtle tones and higher frequencies better than placebo. Clinical trial

Fermented Lion's Mane

  • Double-blind, placebo-controlled study in adults aged 60 to 80 with tinnitus and hearing loss reported improvements in hearing tests. Placebo-controlled study
  • Research suggests lion's mane may support healthy neurotransmitter function and nerve fiber repair. PubMed

Where the ringing comes from

  • Peer-reviewed review describing "central gain": when the ear sends a weaker signal, the auditory system amplifies its own activity, and that amplified internal activity is perceived as phantom ringing. Frontiers in Neurology, 2014 · PubMed 25386157
